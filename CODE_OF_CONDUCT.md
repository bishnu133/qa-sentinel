# Code of conduct

qa-sentinel is a small open-source project for the QA community. We want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, share test automation experience and contribute.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people whose first language is not English.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind; say what to change and why.
- Assume good intent, and say so when something was unclear rather than accusing.
- Keep client data, secrets and personal data out of issues, logs and examples. Remove names, URLs and tokens before you paste output.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, sharing other people's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing so.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately with GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form with the word "conduct" in the title if you need to contact the maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project in public.
